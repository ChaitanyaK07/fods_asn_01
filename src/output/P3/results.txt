Deliverable 7: discussion
-------------------------
The coefficient path (p3_3_coefficient_path.png) shows the L1 penalty driving coefficients into the '~0' band one after another -- 0 ~0 coefficient(s) at lambda = 1e-4, 4 at lambda*, and 8 at lambda = 1 -- whereas at their best lambda Ridge has 1 coefficient(s) with |w_j| < 0.01 against 4 for Lasso (Deliverable 5 table). This follows from the optimality conditions: the L1 subgradient at w_j = 0 is the whole interval [-lambda, lambda], so w_j = 0 is optimal whenever |Phi_j^T r| <= lambda, i.e. once a feature's correlation with the residual falls below lambda the coefficient is held at exactly zero (the SSE contours touch the l1 diamond at a corner on an axis); the L2 penalty's gradient lambda w_j vanishes at w_j = 0, so it only rescales each coefficient towards zero along the circular ball and never sets it exactly to zero. As lambda grows past lambda* = 1.00e-01, more coefficients are removed and the rest are shrunk, so train RMSE rises monotonically (from 0.075 to 0.346 at lambda = 1) and test RMSE also rises (from 0.099 to 0.348): the model moves from a good fit into underfitting, with both errors climbing well above the 0.1 noise level.
